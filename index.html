How I work

Employees forget nothing. A salary that landed a day late, a relieving letter that took a week, a form nobody answered — each one is remembered long after the reason is. So the standard is not a good apology; it is no delay in the first place. Everything I build starts there.

Compliance is designed, not remembered. If a statutory filing depends on someone's memory, it is already a risk. I document ownership per entity and build the calendar into the system.

Finance and Legal are my closest partners. A clean, audit-ready incentive and payroll process is what makes the company's financial reporting true, and every policy, contract and entity registration is written with Legal, not around them.

Systems before policies before programmes. Clean data in one HRIS, then policies that match reality, then the engagement and analytics work that people usually want to start with.

Culture is built in the hard moments. An acquisition, a new leader, a fast-growth year: how people are treated then is the culture, whatever the values poster says.

AI is a working tool, not a slide. I use it daily for reconciliation, drafting and QA, and I want to work in environments that treat it the same way.

Selected work

Manifest Global
Group-wide, 2023 – present

Six entities, one payroll and compliance rhythm

Situation
A group formed from acquisitions — Cialfo (India, US), BridgeU (UK), Kaaiser, Explore Higher Ed, Manifest Singapore — each with its own payroll vendor, calendar and compliance habits.
What I did
Built a single monthly operating rhythm across vendors: shared cut-off calendar, variance checks before every run, one reconciliation view for Finance, and documented statutory ownership per entity. Ran a group-wide handbook compliance review across five jurisdictions, flagging legal exposure in leave, non-compete and data-protection language and getting the policies changed. Audit EOR and payroll vendor invoices as a standing control.
Result
Payroll and statutory filings run to a predictable cadence across six entities with a small team, and each newly acquired entity — most recently BridgeU in the UK in 2025 — is brought onto the same rhythm. Finance closes against one source of truth instead of six.
Kaaiser
2024 – present

A people function from a blank page

Situation
A business unit with employees, managers and ambition, but no HRIS, no written policies, no attendance system and no KPI framework.
What I did
Sequenced the build deliberately: HRIS and clean data first, then attendance and leave, then a policy hub built in Notion and written for how the business actually runs, then KPI and KRA frameworks across eighteen-plus job profiles with leadership. Drafted employment agreements and a three-tier compliance policy framework scoped to Indian employment law and regulatory guidance. Acted as HR business partner to the leadership team throughout.
Result
Leadership makes people decisions from real-time dashboards rather than anecdote, every employee has a policy set that matches their contract, and the unit now has the base for budgeting and incentive work.

Journey

I started at Paytm in 2017 as an Assistant Manager in HR Operations, owning the full employee lifecycle from induction to exit — comp and ben, HRMS, employee experience, and HR audits for internal and external stakeholders. I was there through a year in which we hired around 20,000 people. That is where I learned what operations at scale actually feels like, and what breaks first.

In 2020 I moved to LocoNav because I wanted the global layer. There I set up the Dubai entity, took the company through ISO 27001, implemented Darwinbox from scratch, built the people processes for new business units, designed the tax-saving benefits, ran the performance cycle, automated onboarding, invoicing and investor reporting, and built the people and operations cost model the CEO planned against.

In April 2023 Cialfo came looking for someone to own their India entity end to end. Since then the work has included integrating the talent from two acquired companies, building the IT team from zero, applying AI to how HR runs, and — as Cialfo became Manifest Global — running Talent Operations across six entities and seventeen countries, and partnering with Kaaiser's leadership on budget, incentives, a leadership succession and the culture of a business unit going through change.

MBA. Nine years in HR across fintech, software, ed-tech and logistics-tech, leading teams at every stop — five at Paytm, four at Cialfo. Based in Delhi NCR.
